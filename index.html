From product link to parcel

UIDBuy product links and seller checks

When comparing Weidian product links, Taobao clothing listings or 1688 product listings, keep the original seller URL, marketplace item ID, exact color and size, and the date you checked them. If a link redirects, match the seller name and item photos before adding it to your shopping shortlist. For multipacks, check the minimum order quantity and whether the listing shows a unit or set price.

UIDBuy sizing guide for shoes and clothing

For sneakers, measure both feet in centimeters and use the longer measurement with the seller’s shoe size chart. For a jacket or hoodie, compare chest width, sleeve length and garment length against something you own. For pants and shorts, compare flat waist width and inseam. For caps, check head circumference against the adjustment range.

UIDBuy QC photos and inspection checks

Quality-control (QC) photos help you check the item received at a warehouse. Compare the size tag, color option and any visible damage with your order. For shoes, request outsole photos if needed. For jewelry, check the clasp and bracelet inner circumference. Confirm the seller’s return window before asking for a replacement or approving shipment.

UIDBuy shipping checklist for international orders

Record the item subtotal, domestic delivery, any service fees and the international shipping quote separately. Check whether the carrier bills by actual or volumetric weight. If you plan to consolidate parcels, use the final carton dimensions and packed weight when comparing routes. Check which taxes and import charges are included for your destination.

Frequently asked questions

What is a UIDBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product-discovery list that groups marketplace listings so you can compare options. Use this guide to browse categories on HipoBuyFind and learn what to verify on a seller listing.

Where do the category links go?

They open the corresponding HipoBuyFind categories. Use the destination listing to check availability, variations and current terms.

What should I check in QC photos?

Look for the selected color and size label, relevant measurements, and close-ups of seams, soles or hardware. Ask for another angle if an important detail is hidden.

Do the photos confirm authenticity or availability?

The brand photos illustrate what to look for. For authenticity, check seller provenance and proof of purchase. Confirm availability separately on the current listing.

How should I compare shipping estimates?

Compare quotes for the same destination and package using the final weight, carton dimensions and included fees. Check the carrier’s restrictions and whether import charges are included.
